What Grigri is

Grigri finds you a climbing plan. Make a plan (where, when, which grades), or swipe right on someone else's. Each match opens the plan's group chat, where you sort out the rope, the ride and the coffee. Plans can be:

  • Private (the default): the group lets newcomers in one at a time, when everyone says yes.
  • Open: anyone can join at once.
  • Guided: a guide decides who joins, and sets a price per climber, paid to the guide, not through Grigri.

Grigri is free for every climber. Premium is optional, for advertising on Grigri and for the route finder.

Getting started

  • Sign in with Google (or with Apple on iPhone, being added). Your profile starts with your name.
  • On the Me tab (your profile), tap Edit profile to add a photo, your grades and your gear, and set where you climb, so you see plans near you first. Where you climb is private: only you see it.
  • On Find, swipe right on a plan to ask to join, or left to pass. ⟳ brings back plans you passed.
  • In Plans, tap New plan to make your own.

Staying safe, and reporting someone

Meet new partners at the crag or the gym, check each other's knots and belay, and climb within your limits.

If someone or something on Grigri is abusive, offensive, a scam or otherwise breaks the rules:

  • In the app (being added): report a person, a plan or a message, and block a person so you no longer see them or their plans.
  • By email: write to julio@nu01.com with the person's name, the plan, and what happened (a screenshot helps).

We review every report within 24 hours, remove content that breaks the rules, and block the accounts that post it.

Premium

Premium costs $20 a month or $200 a year, and lets you advertise on Grigri and use the route finder. Everything else is the same for everyone.

  • Bought through Stripe (in the browser): to cancel, change your card or see invoices, go to Me → Grigri Premium → Manage subscription, which opens Stripe's billing portal. Premium stays until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Bought in the iPhone app (Apple in-app purchase, being added): Apple manages it. Cancel in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Grigri. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Me → Delete account (being added to the app). Or email julio@nu01.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days. Cancel Premium first, as above. See the privacy policy for what's deleted.

Signing out

On the Me tab, tap ⋯, then Sign out, or Sign out everywhere to sign out on every phone and browser.

# Screens

Five tabs, in a bottom navigation bar: Discover, Plans, Chats,
Notifications and Profile. The labels fit a 360 dp wide phone.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | The plans deck ([matching](matching.md)), with ✕ and ✓ buttons. When it runs out: "You've seen every plan" and **New plan**. |
| **Plans** | The plans I'm in, soonest first (discipline, place, date, the group's avatars and size, "Organizing" or "Accepted", "N to review"), then **Asked to join**: "Pending" and who it's waiting for, or "Declined" with ✕ to dismiss. **New plan** (a floating button). |
| **Chats** | One group chat per plan I'm in, most recent first, with the last message and "N to answer" when join requests wait for my answer. The tab's badge counts those requests. |
| **Notifications** | Join requests for my plans, and the groups' answers to mine ([matching](matching.md#notifications)). The tab's badge counts the unread ones. |
| **Profile** | My profile as others see it: my photo (tap to see it whole), name and age, about, climbs, and gear with its photos (tap one to see it whole); **Edit profile**; in ⋯, Reset the demo data. The version at the bottom. |

- **Plan** (from Plans, a notification, or a chat's title): the plan
  highlighted, my request's status when I'm not in it yet (Pending, N of
  M said yes, or Declined), the group (tap someone for their profile),
  who's asking to join and how many have said yes ("Answer in the chat",
  or "You said yes"). Members get
  **Group chat** and **Leave the plan** (Cancel the plan when I'm alone in
  it), after a confirmation. The organizer can **Edit**.
- **Plan editor**: title, discipline, crag or gym, area, date and start
  time, length (1–12 h), grades (a range on the discipline's scale,
  starting at my own grades), group size, notes. Title and place are
  required.
- **Profile editor**: my photo (tap it: take a photo, where the device has
  a camera, choose one, or remove it), name, age (optional, 16–99
  when given), about, per
  discipline a switch and a grade range, and gear: the gear chips, then
  its photos (✕ removes one, + adds one, up to 6).
- **Match**: see [matching](matching.md#matches).
- **Chat**: the group's avatars, the plan's title and date, the plan pinned
  under the bar, messages (mine on the right in pine, others' on the left
  with their name), the join and leave notes, and a message box.
- Tapping a person anywhere opens their whole profile in a sheet.

The browser tab's title is **Grigri** (**🧪 Grigri RC** on the RC site).
The look is the app's [theme](theme.md).

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

In short. Grigri keeps what it needs to match you with climbing plans and to run their group chats, and nothing more.

We don't track you, we don't sell or share your data for advertising, and the app has no third-party ad or analytics SDKs.

You can delete your account at any time.

Grigri is an app for finding climbing plans and the people in them. It runs on iPhone, Android and the web at grigri.nu01.com. This policy covers all three. In it, "Grigri", "we" and "us" mean the people who run Grigri. You can reach us at julio@nu01.com.

What we collect, and why

Your account

  • Signing in with Google: Google tells us your Google account ID, your name and your email address. Your name starts your profile. Your account ID links you to your profile.
  • Signing in with Apple (being added to the iPhone app): Apple tells us your Apple account ID, your email address (or a private relay address Apple makes for you, if you choose that) and, the first time only, your name.
  • Your email address is never shown on your profile and never given to other climbers. We use it to look up your roles in Grigri (for example whether you're an administrator or have Premium) with our own role service, and for Premium billing.
  • Sessions: when you sign in, the app gets a random session token, which it keeps in your device's secure storage. We keep only a fingerprint (hash) of it, so we can recognise your device until you sign out.

Your profile

What you choose to add: a photo, your name, your age (optional; it must be 16 or over), a short bio, your grades per discipline, the gear you can bring, and photos of your gear. Other climbers see your profile when you ask to join their plans, or when they look at the members of a plan you're in.

Where you climb

Your location and up to five other areas you climb in. If your profile has no location, the app asks your phone or browser where it is, once, with the system's permission prompt. Before saving, the app rounds it to about 1 km. Where you climb is private: only you see it. We use it to show you plans near you first.

Plans, requests and chats

  • Plans you make: what, where, when, for which grades, who can join (and a price, for a guided plan), and notes. Plans are shown to other climbers on Find (Discover).
  • Join requests: which plans you asked to join, and the group's answers. Your swipes to pass on a plan are kept so we don't show it again.
  • Group chat messages, and the photos and videos you send in them, which the plan's members see.
  • Plan photos that you add to a plan, which its members see.

Premium

Premium is an optional subscription for advertising on Grigri and for the route finder.

  • Payments through Stripe: you pay on Stripe's own pages. Grigri never sees or stores your card number. Stripe tells us your subscription's status, plan and renewal date, and we keep those with Stripe's customer and subscription IDs and your email address.
  • Payments through Apple (in-app purchase, being added to the iPhone app): Apple takes the payment under its own terms. Apple tells us the subscription's status and renewal date. We never see your payment details.
  • Ads that Premium climbers make: the text, the link, and the photo or video. Once an administrator approves an ad, other climbers see it on Find with the advertiser's name.
  • The route finder: routes that you record, and photos of the rock walls they're on, so other climbers can see the routes. The camera's live picture is processed on your phone and isn't uploaded.

Match scores

To judge how likely a climber and a plan are to match, our server works out a few numbers: whether your grades fit the plan, how far it is, how big the group is, how many people are waiting, and how soon it starts. It sends only those numbers to aityx, a decision service. aityx works out a score and keeps nothing. Your name, age, photos, bio and location never go to aityx. For now, only administrators see the scores.

What your device keeps

The app keeps a few settings on your device, such as your language and grade system. On phones it also keeps a developer log file in its own storage. The log never leaves your device unless you send it to us yourself, and it's scrubbed of email addresses and sign-in tokens.

The website

The landing page at grigri.nu01.com can show crags near you. It works out the distances in your browser, so your location stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Services that process data for us

  • Amazon Web Services hosts Grigri: the website, the app's server, the database, the photo store and live updates.
  • Google and Apple sign you in.
  • Stripe and Apple take Premium payments.
  • aityx works out match scores from numbers that don't identify you (see above).

We don't share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Where it's stored

On Amazon Web Services in the United States (the us-east-1 region, in Northern Virginia). Data travels encrypted (HTTPS), and AWS encrypts it at rest. Photos and videos are served through Grigri's own site.

Live updates: while the app is open, it holds a connection to our server (AWS IoT Core) to hear about new messages and requests at once. Each notice says only what changed (for example "a new message in this plan"), never the message itself, and only you can listen to your own notices.

Logs: our server logs errors, without bodies or account data, and keeps them 14 days.

How long we keep it

  • Your profile, plans and messages: until you delete them or your account.
  • Photos and videos sent in chats, and plan photos: 400 days, then they're deleted.
  • Photos you upload but don't use: one day.
  • Sign-in sessions: until you sign out, or after 365 days without use.
  • Match scores: until the day after the plan starts.
  • Server error logs: 14 days.
  • Backups: our database keeps a rolling 35-day backup, so deleted data leaves the backups within 35 days.
  • Billing records: as long as the law requires.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Me → Delete account (being added to the app). Or email julio@nu01.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Deleting your account deletes it and the personal data linked to it, except what the law requires us to keep, such as billing records. Backups forget it within 35 days.

If you have Premium, cancel it too: one bought through Stripe in Me → Grigri Premium → Manage subscription, one bought through Apple in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions, since Apple manages it.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your profile at any time in the app.
  • Leave out what's optional: your photo, age, bio, grades, gear and where you climb.
  • Turn location access off in your device's settings. You can still pick a place by hand.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by email. Depending on where you live (for example in the European Union, the UK, Brazil or California), the law may give you more rights, such as to object or to complain to your data protection authority. We'll honour them.

What we don't do

  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell your data, or share it for advertising.
  • The app has no third-party ad, analytics or tracking SDKs. The only ads in Grigri are those Premium climbers make, and they aren't targeted at you.

Children

Grigri is not for anyone under 16. Profiles take ages from 16 up. If you believe a child under 16 uses Grigri, write to us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll also tell you in the app before it applies.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: julio@nu01.com.

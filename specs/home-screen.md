# Home screen

The app opens on a single static screen, centered:

- the title, **Grigri** (**🧪 Grigri RC** on the release-candidate site);
- the tagline, "Find a partner to climb with";
- the version, `X.Y.Z`, in small text, when the build has one (builds
  through the repository's scripts do; a bare `flutter run` or a test
  doesn't).

Light or dark follows the system, with an orange color scheme (seed
`#F07020`). The browser tab's title is the same as the screen's.

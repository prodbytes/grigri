# Screens

Five tabs, in a bottom navigation bar:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Discover** | The plans deck ([matching](matching.md)), with ✕ and ✓ buttons. When it runs out: "You've seen every plan" and **New plan**. |
| **Requests** | The people deck: who asked to join plans I'm in. The tab's badge counts them. Empty: what it's for. |
| **Plans** | The plans I'm in, soonest first (discipline, place, date, the group's avatars and size, "Organizing", "N to review"), then the ones waiting for a group's yes, and who it's waiting for. **New plan** (a floating button). |
| **Chats** | One group chat per plan I'm in, most recent first, with the last message. |
| **Profile** | My profile as others see it, **Edit profile**, and, in ⋯, Reset the demo data. The version at the bottom. |

- **Plan** (from Plans, or a chat's title): the plan highlighted, the
  group (tap someone for their profile), who's asking to join and how many
  have said yes, **Group chat**, and **Leave the plan** (Cancel the plan
  when I'm alone in it), after a confirmation. The organizer can **Edit**.
- **Plan editor**: title, discipline, crag or gym, area, date and start
  time, length (1–12 h), grades (a range on the discipline's scale,
  starting at my own grades), group size, notes. Title and place are
  required.
- **Profile editor**: name, age (16–99), years climbing, home area, about,
  per discipline a switch and a grade range, a weekday × part-of-day grid
  for when, places to add and remove, and gear.
- **Match**: see [matching](matching.md#matches).
- **Chat**: the group's avatars, the plan's title and date, the plan pinned
  under the bar, messages (mine on the right in pine, others' on the left
  with their name), the join and leave notes, and a message box.
- Tapping a person anywhere opens their whole profile in a sheet.

The browser tab's title is **Grigri** (**🧪 Grigri RC** on the RC site).
The look is the app's [theme](theme.md).
